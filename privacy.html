Privacy Policy

This page explains how MD Service Work collects and uses application information.

What we collect

When you submit the partner form we collect: name, age, city, WhatsApp number, email, occupation, the types of local businesses you can reach, how you plan to bring clients, and how you heard about MD Service.

How we use it

We use this information only to review partner applications, contact you about the program, and keep an internal record of referrals and commission status.

Who receives it

Application details are sent to MD Service at mdservice.in@gmail.com and stored in MD Service’s private internal record. The public website does not display applications or contact numbers of other partners.

Contact

Questions: mdservice.in@gmail.com
