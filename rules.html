Partner Rules

Team Member / Partner Rules

Yeh program referral / team opportunity hai. Isse MD Service ki ownership, equity, business rights ya management rights nahi milte.

  1. Minimum age 16 years.
  2. Joining is free.
  3. No special skill is required.
  4. Partner's primary role is to bring clients.
  5. MD Service decides final pricing.
  6. Partner cannot independently promise pricing, discounts or terms on behalf of MD Service.
  7. Client must make a one-time full payment.
  8. Commission is payable only after full client payment is successfully received.
  9. If the client does not pay, no commission is payable.
  10. If the project/client cancels before successful full payment, no commission is payable.
  11. Same client ko multiple partners refer karne ki situation mein, MD Service ke recorded submission ke according sabse pehle valid client submission karne wale partner ko priority di jayegi.
  12. Partner has no ownership or equity in MD Service.
  13. Partner does not receive management or business rights in MD Service.
  14. MD Service handles project development and delivery.
  15. Commission is 20% of the actual amount received from the client, subject to the applicable program rules.

Partner kya nahi karega

  • Website build nahi karega
  • Project pricing decide nahi karega
  • Permission ke bina discount promise nahi karega
  • Timelines independently decide nahi karega
  • MD Service ke naam se project payment collect nahi karega
  • Khud ko MD Service ka owner nahi bataega
