By submitting a partner application you agree to these terms and the Partner Rules.

MD Service Work is the partner/referral program of MD Service. Joining is free and does not create employment, partnership-in-ownership, equity, or management rights.

The partner’s role is to introduce potential local-business clients who need a website. MD Service handles pricing, communication after handover, development, delivery, and payment collection.

Commission is 20% of the actual amount successfully received from the client after one-time full payment. If the client does not pay, does not complete payment, or cancels before successful full payment, no commission is payable.

If two or more partners refer the same client, the earliest valid recorded submission with MD Service gets priority.

Partners must not collect project payments, promise prices or discounts, or present themselves as owners of MD Service.
