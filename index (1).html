Partner Rules — Clear Boundaries

Team member / partner hona ownership nahi hai. Pricing, project aur payment MD Service handle karta hai.

  1. Minimum age 16 years. Joining free hai.
  2. Partner ka primary role clients lana hai — website banana nahi.
  3. Final pricing MD Service decide karta hai. Partner khud se discount ya rate promise nahi kar sakta.
  4. Client ko one-time full payment karni hoti hai. Commission tabhi eligible hota hai jab full payment successfully receive ho.
  5. Agar client pay nahi kare, cancel kare, ya payment complete na ho — commission nahi milega.
  6. Same client ko multiple partners refer karne ki situation mein, MD Service ke recorded submission ke according sabse pehle valid client submission karne wale partner ko priority di jayegi.
  7. Is program se MD Service ki ownership, equity, management rights ya owner ki tarah represent karne ka adhikar nahi milta.

FAQ

Is joining MD Service free?

Yes. There is no joining fee.

What is the minimum age?

16 years.

Do I need any special skill?

No.

What exactly do I have to do?

Your main role is to bring potential clients to MD Service.

How much commission do I get?

20% of the actual amount paid by the client, subject to the program rules.

Is there a limit on the number of clients?

There is no fixed client limit.

When do I receive commission?

After the client's full payment is successfully received by MD Service.

Can the client pay in installments?

No. MD Service requires one-time full payment for these projects.

Who decides the project price?

MD Service.

Who builds the website?

MD Service.

Do I get ownership of MD Service?

No. Joining the program does not provide ownership, equity or business rights.

What happens if the client doesn't pay?

No commission is payable.

What if two partners bring the same client?

The earliest valid recorded client submission gets priority.
